# Privacy

Kitchtally is built so your data stays with you.

- **No internet.** Kitchtally contains no networking code. On Android it doesn't even request the internet permission, and the build fails if that ever changes.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.**
- **Stored on your phone only.** Items, purchases, prices and shops live in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled.
- **Photos** you take or choose are resized and copied into the app's private storage. Kitchtally doesn't keep access to your gallery, and on Android it doesn't ask for camera permission: your camera app takes the picture.
- **Backups** are files you create and keep yourself. They contain your items and history in plain text, without photos, so store them somewhere private.

Uninstalling Kitchtally deletes all of its data.
