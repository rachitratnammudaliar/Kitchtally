# Privacy

Duedial is built so your data stays with you, on Android and iPhone.

- **No internet.** Duedial contains no networking code on any platform. On Android it doesn't even request the internet permission, and the build fails if that ever changes.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.**
- **Card details.** Duedial stores only a name you choose (for example "HDFC Visa") and the last four digits. It never asks for or stores a full card number, expiry date or CVV.
- **Backups** are files you create and keep yourself. They contain your subscriptions in plain text, so store them somewhere private.
- **Notifications** are created on the device. No server is involved.

## Where your data is stored

| Phone | Location |
|---|---|
| Android | A private database other apps can't read. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Duedial |
| iPhone, iPad | A file inside the app's own private storage, protected by iOS data protection while the phone is locked |

On Android, custom icons you choose are copied, resized, into the app's private storage, and Duedial doesn't keep access to your gallery.

Uninstalling Duedial deletes all of its data.
