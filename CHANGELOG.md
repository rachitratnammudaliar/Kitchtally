# Changelog

## 1.12.0
- **Week agenda:** a new button on the home page. Plan the next 7 days by picking what you'll use each day. The agenda shows what you won't have enough of for the week and adds it to your shopping list in one tap ("Add all to shopping list"). It stays on your phone and isn't part of backups. Android and iPhone together.

## 1.11.0
- **The home page now shows what you have:** an "Available" list (low stock and soon-to-expire first, then A–Z) with filter chips: All, Running low, Expiring soon, Long-lasting and your categories. Things you've run out of sit in a collapsed "Out of stock" section. Long-lasting things stay out of "All" until you pick their chip or search.
- **Best-before dates:** add one when you buy (+3 days, +1 week, +1 month or pick a date). Items show "Best before in 3 days" and a "Use first" strip appears for anything expiring within 3 days. Expired? Tap Threw away to take it out of stock.
- **Reminders:** Settings → Reminders sends a notification at 9:00, 2 days before (or 0–7 days, your choice). It stays on your phone; the app still has no internet access.
- Database moves to version 10; your data and backups carry over. Android and iPhone together.

## 1.10.2
- The full list is back under the category tiles on the home page: scroll down to see every item, grouped by category, like before. Tap a tile to jump to just that category.

## 1.10.1
- iPhone gets everything from 1.10.0: the category-tiles home page with the "Needs buying" strip, long-lasting items (learned duration, run-out date, cost a month, best-before, "Long-lasting?" suggestion), best shop per item, the new shops (Depo, Silas) with hide in Settings, the Frozen and Health categories with Tidy categories, and optional cooking features (Settings → Features).

## 1.10.0
- **New home page:** tap a category tile (🥬 Vegetables, 🥛 Dairy & eggs, 🪥 Long-lasting…). A "Needs buying" strip shows what's on To buy, with the names.
- **Long-lasting items:** mark any item as long-lasting (toothpaste, vitamins, detergent). It stays off the weekly bill and is tracked on its own. Say about how many days one purchase lasts; after two purchases the app learns your real figure, and buying two at once counts for twice as long. It shows when it runs low (with an Add to shop button), what it costs a month, and an optional best-before date. Items you buy about once a month or less get a "Long-lasting?" suggestion.
- **Best shop** per item, from what you paid at each shop. New shops: Depo and Silas. Hide shops you don't use in Settings.
- **Cooking features are optional:** Recipes, the meal planner and Cook a meal are off by default (Settings → Features). Your recipes are kept.
- New categories: Frozen and Health. "Tidy categories" in Settings files items sitting in Other.
- Database moves to version 9; your data and backups carry over. Android first, iPhone follows.

## 1.9.1
- iPhone gets everything from 1.9.0: Not this week and breaks, Skipping section, skip all that's left, the new-week review, Treats & fun, the What's new note and the backup reminder. (Also includes Recipes and the meal planner, as in 1.8.0.)

## 1.9.0
- **Not this week:** on To buy, "More" lets you skip an item until the next week starts, or take a break for 2 weeks, 4 weeks, or until you add it back. Skipped items wait in their own "Skipping" section with an Add back button, and don't count in the badge, the estimate or Done shopping. Pantry shows a quiet "skipping" tag instead of a low-stock warning. "Skip all that's left" does the whole list in one tap.
- **New week review:** when a new week starts, To buy asks "Still want these?" so you can switch off what you don't feel like buying.
- **Treats & fun:** a new category (chocolate, soda, sweets). Items in it only come up on To buy from Thursday, ready for the weekend, unless you mark them as needing a refill.
- A **What's new** note appears once after each update, and a **backup reminder** appears after a month without one.
- Android first: iPhone follows in 1.9.1.
- Database moves to version 8; your data and backups carry over.

## 1.8.0
- New **Recipes** tab between Pantry and To buy. Add a dish, pick its ingredients from your pantry, mark each Needed or Optional, and set the usual amount. One cook makes 4 meals for you, or 2 if you share (you can change both numbers).
- Recipes are coloured by what you have: green can cook now, amber is only missing something optional, red is missing a needed item. "What can I cook?" filters to what's possible. Each recipe shows about how much a meal costs, from your last prices.
- Tap a recipe to cook it: change the amounts (25 / 50 / 75 / All, or type) and the stock goes down by that much. Undo is available. Missing items can be added to To buy from there.
- **Meal planner:** plan a recipe for a day or "this week". What's missing for planned meals shows at the top of To buy, with the reason ("for Chole · Tue"). Meals you don't plan add nothing.
- To buy now has **ASAP, Priority and Anytime** sections. Use "More" on an item to move it. They reset to Priority once you buy the item.
- Database moves to version 7; your data and backups carry over.

## 1.7.0
- New "Bought on a craving" switch on a purchase. Weekly shows how much went on cravings (could have skipped).
- New "No need to buy again" switch on an item. It never appears on the shopping list and sits in its own group at the bottom of Pantry.
- Database moves to version 6; your data and backups carry over (older backups still restore).

## 1.6.0

**New**
- **Use part of one:** for packets, bottles and tubes, the Use box has **25% · 50% · 75% · All** and an **Other %** field. No more typing 0.5
- **Amounts read like people say them:** "½ packet left", "1½ packets", "Used a little", never 0.75 or 0.1
- **Start new week:** a button on Weekly. Today becomes day one of the new week, so a shop you do on the last day counts in the new week. After **Done shopping** late in the week, the app offers it. Undo is one tap
- **Report a problem** in Settings: opens your share menu with the app version, your phone model and the last error. Nothing is sent by itself

**Changed**
- Long-lasting things (face cream, perfume, dishwashing liquid) show only what you paid, in their own small section on Weekly
- The Spent card only says "more than the week before" when there was a week before
- The bars in the Spent card are plain bars again
- The "Counts in" setting on purchases is gone; Start new week replaces it

## 1.5.1

- **Week starts on:** choose any day, in Settings

## 1.3.0

**New**
- **% left:** count bread, oil, ketchup and opened packets as "80% left" instead of "1 packet". Tap the amount to pick 100 / 75 / 50 / 25% / Empty or type any %
- **Grams and millilitres**, still priced per kg and per litre
- **Meals:** "Count meals" shows "4 pieces left · about 6 meals" and turns red when one meal is left. Set it on the item or when you buy ("these make about 8 meals"). Running low can be set in meals (e.g. 2 meals) or in % (e.g. 10% for oil)
- **🍳 Cook a meal:** tick what you cooked with; one meal comes off each in one tap
- **"Almost out" line** under items at their low point, no notifications
- **Shopping list by shop**, with ticks and **Done shopping** to log everything at once, and **Share** to send the list as text
- **Price check:** shows when another shop was cheaper last time
- **Home screen widget** (Android): To buy count and what's almost out
- **Optional monthly budget** under Weekly → Show spending

**Changed**
- Money is out of sight: the Pantry no longer shows what you spent, and Weekly opens with what's new this week. Spending is one tap away
- How long things last: Short is 2 to 3 days, Medium 1 week to 10 days, Long 2 weeks or more (no freshness reminder)
- New items fill in better: onions, potatoes, carrots and tomatoes in pieces; bread, oil, sauces, milk and spices in %; meals counted for vegetables, pasta, rice, eggs and meat
- Items counted in % or meals only say "almost out" at their own low point, never earlier
